    What happens when you search

    1. Understand. A fast model detects the language and dialect, routes requests for rulings, and writes an MSA rewrite plus two paraphrases in Classical Quranic vocabulary. The raw query is kept for ranking.
    2. Retrieve. Keyword search (BM25) over the ayah text and Tafsir al-Muyassar for all 6,236 ayat, plus embedding search on the raw query and the rewrite. All lists are merged with Reciprocal Rank Fusion; the top 40 go forward. Keyword search never runs on raw dialect text.
    3. Rank. A strong model reads each candidate with its tafsir and returns only ayah IDs, a grade (direct, implicit or story, related) and a one-line reason, or “no relevant ayah”.
    4. Guard. IDs outside the candidates are dropped, and any reason that reproduces 5 or more consecutive words of the Quran is withheld.
    5. Render. Ayat are shown by ID from the Tanzil Uthmani text, never from model output.
    Quran text: Tanzil Project, Uthmani v1.1 (tanzil.net), shown verbatim under CC BY 3.0. Tafsir: Al-Tafsir al-Muyassar (King Fahd Complex). Results are an aid to reflection, not a religious ruling.
